🛒 Supermart Grocery Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

📌 Project Overview
The Supermart Grocery Sales Analysis Dashboard is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to analyze grocery sales, profitability, product performance, and geographic trends. The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights using Excel data analysis tools, PivotTables, charts.
The main goal is to understand business performance, identify profitable product categories and locations, and highlight areas where high sales do not necessarily translate into higher profit margins.

🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales, profit, and profit margin.
- Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Compare sales and profitability across geographic locations.
- Investigate products and locations with high sales but comparatively low profit margins.
- Examine the relationship between discount and profit.
- Create an interactive dashboard to support data-driven business decisions.

🛠️ Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Combo charts and scatter plots
- Excel formulas and calculated columns
- Correlation and trendline analysis
- Data visualization and dashboard design

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard highlights the following key performance indicators:
- Total Sales: Approximately $14.96 million
- Total Profit: Approximately $3.75 million
- Overall Profit Margin: Approximately 25%

These KPIs provide a high-level overview of the business's sales and profitability.

🔍 Analysis Performed
1. Overall Sales Performance
Analyzed total sales and profit to understand the overall business performance and profitability.

2. Product and Category Performance
Compared sales and profit across product categories and sub-categories to identify high-performing product groups and areas where profitability could be improved.
3. Profitability Analysis
Calculated profit margins to evaluate how effectively sales translate into profit. Products with high sales but comparatively low profit margins can be investigated for potential improvement.
4. Geographic Performance
Analyzed sales, profit, and profit margins across cities and other geographic groupings. This helps identify locations that generate substantial sales but have comparatively lower profitability.
5. Discount vs. Profit Analysis
Used a scatter plot and linear trendline to investigate the relationship between discount and profit.

The analysis produced an R-squared value of 0.0132, indicating that the fitted linear relationship explains approximately 1.32% of the variation in profit in the plotted data. The trendline has a positive slope, but the relationship is very weak.

This suggests that discount alone is not a strong linear predictor of profit in this analysis. Other factors, such as product category, sales volume, and pricing, should also be considered.

💡 Key Insights
- The business generated approximately $14.96 million in sales and $3.75 million in profit.
- The overall profit margin is approximately 25%.
- Sales and profitability vary across product categories, sub-categories, and geographic locations.
- Some locations meet the selected criteria for high sales and comparatively low profit margins, making them candidates for further investigation.
- The discount-profit analysis indicates a very weak positive linear relationship in the plotted data, with an R-squared value of 0.0132.
- Sales performance should be evaluated alongside profit and profit margin rather than revenue alone.
